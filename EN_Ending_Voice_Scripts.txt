If you leave now, you'll never be free again.

The house won't let you go, and you'll end up like us.

I can't tell you what happened to us.

Find the right way out.
Free us all.

ENDING III — YOU ARE FREE! FOR NOW!
The full German spoken narration for ending III was not available for a faithful translation. Its on-screen ending title and descriptive text have been translated in the game.

NOTE: These are translation scripts, not synthesized voice recordings. The English loop voice file romina-loop-ending-en.wav must be present alongside the HTML if the spoken English loop message is to play. The HTML references external audio files, which are not embedded in this package.
